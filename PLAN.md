# samsvaibhab Private Limited — Business Plan

## Business Idea

Nepal has many regions that grow different crops in different quantities and qualities — for example, apples grow especially well in Mustang, and cabbages grow especially well in Chitwan. A large amount of this produce currently goes to waste, despite there being significant demand for these same products abroad.

samsvaibhab will act as the connector between these two sides. For example, if a supplier in Dubai wants 100kg of apples, and there is a farmer in Nepal who can supply 100kg of apples, samsvaibhab will source those apples, sell them to the buyer abroad, pay the farmer their earnings, and take a commission on the transaction.

As a first step, market research will be done to identify the most-traded and most-exported items from Nepal to determine what to list on the platform.

## Product: The Website

The website will connect two businessmen (Nepali suppliers and international buyers) and should be kept simple.

### Account Types

1. **Supplier accounts** — for suppliers based in Nepal. Can be individual farmers or companies. There is no minimum scale requirement — e.g., a single farmer with 100kg of a product can list and sell through the platform.

2. **Buyer accounts** — for buyers based abroad (e.g., companies in Dubai). Buyers post a request specifying what product and quantity they need (e.g., apples, oranges). These requests are made visible/available on the platform.

### Core Flow

- Buyers submit requests for specific products and quantities.
- The platform matches these requests with supplier availability.
- samsvaibhab facilitates the sale, pays the supplier their earnings, and takes a commission.

## The rule that never breaks: the two sides never meet

**A buyer never learns who the supplier is. A supplier never learns who the
buyer is. Not ever.**

samsvaibhab is the only point of contact for both of them. The buyer is our
customer. The supplier is our customer. They are not each other's customer.

This is not a setting or a preference. It is the shape of the whole platform,
and everything is built so that it cannot be broken by accident:

- The two sides have no way to reach each other. There is no shared screen, no
  messaging between them, no shared document, no shared contact detail.
- A buyer's request does not carry the buyer's name, company, country or
  contact. A supplier's answer does not carry the supplier's name, farm,
  district or contact.
- Anything either side uploads is seen by us, never passed across.
- Prices are ours to set on each side. The supplier is told what we pay them.
  The buyer is told what we charge them. Neither sees the other number.

## Admin is the control room

Nothing passes between the two sides on its own. Everything goes through us.

```

                      decide who to ask              we will pay

   the offer          strip the supplier's           this is my price
```

The admin dashboard is where every one of those decisions is made. It is not a
screen for watching the platform — it *is* the platform. Approvals, matching,
pricing, transport, risk, insurance and payment all happen there.

## Room to grow

Nepal has a very large number of products that could be sold this way, and each
one is described differently — apples have a variety and a grade, lentils have a
size, spices have a moisture level. The platform is built so that a new product,
and the details that come with it, can be added without a developer rewriting
anything.

The same is true for the people who work in the control room. A risk analyst, an
accounts person and a transport coordinator each see only the part of the
control room that is their job. New jobs can be added as the business grows.
